# DEMO · How to Write a Good Video Script · B-roll list

Made up for the demo script. "Over" says which spoken line the shot covers.

| # | What to show | Over (A-roll line) | How to make it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B1 | Blank page, blinking cursor, a clock | "A blank page. A deadline…" | Stock clip or a staged desk shot |
| B2 | A wall of blog-style text; the viewer scrolls away | "Most of us write a video like a blog post." | Screen capture of a long made-up post |
| B3 | Two first lines: one crossed out, one ticked | "Not "In this video I will…"" | Motion plate |
| B4 | One long sentence splits into three short ones | "A script is not read…" through "…show it on screen." | Motion graphic |
| B5 | Key icon: a backup is a spare key | "A backup is a spare key." | Slide |
| B6 | Checklist with three ticks | "Keep one checklist next to every script." | Photo of a printed checklist |
| B7 | The voice check count in the top bar | "Mark those lines…" | Screenshot of the editor |
| B8 | Three ticks build up one by one | "So, three things." | Motion plate |
