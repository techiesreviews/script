Take-over fix round (SL-1 and SL-2), 2026-11-20:
The first builder ran out of fix rounds with SL-1 (P1) and SL-2 (P2) open. Both came from one cause: history steps
ran out of order with the state they were recorded against (stacks changed at once, shot writes ran later, each step
compared quotes it had kept). Tracking where every generated quote came from, and re-ordering dependent steps after
a failure, would have added more state to that. The design was simplified instead:
- History takes turns. A move's save, and any Undo or Redo pressed while one is under way, each wait for the one
  before and then meet the script and the list as that one left them. A script-words step with nothing saving still
  happens at once, as before.
- A step that cannot be done safely stops there. A save that fails, or a new change, drops the Undo and Redo presses
  still waiting; the failed step keeps its place and can be tried again. No words are undone by mistake.
- A step finds the shot by where it shows in the open script (the paragraphs it covers), not by comparing kept quotes;
  only when it shows on no paragraph, or the list was changed outside the page, by where its saved quote matches.
This removed the kept copy of the paragraphs, the expected-quote and same-place checks, and the cross-stack cancel.

SL-1 (recorded rewrite between two moves): met. The unit regression moves A to B, records a full rewrite of B, moves
B to C, then undoes and redoes each step with a wait. It failed first at 4fb1a7f ("the third Undo reaches the first
paragraph"). The local browser check now does the same by real drag, typing and Ctrl+Z at 1440 and 390 px, light and
dark; run against the files of 4fb1a7f it failed with "timed out: third Undo reaches the first paragraph".
SL-2 (failed Undo save with an Undo waiting): met. The unit regression moves A to B to C, presses Undo twice while
the first Undo's save is held, fails that save, then retries. It failed first at 4fb1a7f ("both moves keep their
Undo, in their order": 1 step left instead of 2). Now nothing moves, both steps stay in order, the retry reaches B,
the next Undo reaches A, and both Redos work.
Also covered: typing while an Undo saves stops the Undo waiting behind it; an Undo pressed while a move's first save
fails undoes nothing; a shot shown on no paragraph for a moment is found by its saved quote. The fixture now keeps
Check flags across writes as the app does, so "the new drag clears Check" tests the real line (Opus nit, round 6).
Local safety: throwaway scripts in fresh private browser contexts on 127.0.0.1 only; no live editor, no real script,
no agent connection. The local server and Chrome were kit bg jobs and were stopped.
